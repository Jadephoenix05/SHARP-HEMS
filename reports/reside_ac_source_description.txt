The RESIDE-AC dataset is available as well-structured and easy-to-use
.CSV (Comma Separated Values) files. The data set contains 19 summer days data
from 10/05/2021 IST to 28/05/2021 IST for 8 single-AC homes and 3 homes with
more than one AC. Garud and Envilog captured datetime values in IST (Indian
Standard Time) which can be converted to UTC by subtracting 5:30 hours. Each
home has 27360 Garud records and 5472 Envilog records.
